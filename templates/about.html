Dual-Phase Learning Protocol

We don't just train; we adapt. Our system utilizes a sophisticated Two-Stage Transfer Learning approach that leverages the massive feature-extraction power of ImageNet and fine-tunes it for the subtleties of neuroimaging.

Phase 1: Feature Extraction

Frozen Base

The Convolutional Base (Deep CNN) is locked to preserve learned filters. We only train the custom top-level classifier to map general features to tumor classes.

Result: 91.36% Accuracy

Phase 2: Fine-Tuning

Active

We unfreeze the deepest 20 Layers of the network. This allows the model to adjust its high-level abstract representations specifically for MRI textures.

Result: 98.39% Accuracy
